Shipping & returns

We want your Kota Kurtis order to reach you beautifully and feel just right.

Shipping: Orders are dispatched within 2–4 business days. Delivery usually takes 5–8 business days after dispatch.

Delivery fee: Shipping is free above ₹1,499. A ₹99 delivery charge applies to smaller orders.

Size exchange: Request an exchange within 7 days of delivery. The product must be unused, unwashed and have original tags.

Damaged or incorrect item: Share photos or an unboxing video on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. We will help resolve it quickly.
